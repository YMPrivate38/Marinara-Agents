## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-22
- Added: Fanfic Writer can now use web search to check a canon fact when your character cards and lorebook do not settle it. It treats results as unverified hints. Turn the tool off in the agent's settings if you do not want searches.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-22
- Changed: Fanfic Writer now checks each Roleplay reply for out-of-character writing and things that don't make sense, and rewrites the reply when a score falls below the minimum, instead of planning chapters. Chapter length, point of view and tense moved to Story Writer.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-22
- Added: Fanfic Writer plans each Roleplay reply as one complete chapter of serial fiction, with a chapter length, point of view and tense you can change. Add it in Chat Settings → Agents → Writer Agents.
